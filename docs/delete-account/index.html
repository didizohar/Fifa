Delete Your Account

Couch League account deletion is self-service and happens entirely inside the app. You do not need to contact support to delete your account.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Couch League and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the "Danger Zone" section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, exactly as shown on screen.
  5. Your account is deleted immediately. You are signed out and returned to the login screen.
One exception: you're the sole owner of a shared group

If you are the only owner of a group that still has other members in it, deletion is blocked with a clear message asking you to transfer ownership or remove the other members first. This prevents your account deletion from silently orphaning a group your friends are still using.

What happens when you delete your account

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Specifically:

  • Your login credentials (email and password) are permanently deleted. You will not be able to sign back in with this account.
  • Any group where you were the sole owner and sole member is permanently deleted along with it, including its uploaded group avatar image.
  • Your membership in any other group is removed.
  • Your player profile (display name, stats, Elo rating, match history) is not deleted — it is unlinked from your login instead. This is so that recorded matches, rankings, and history your group depends on are not disrupted for the other members who played those matches with you. Your player profile becomes an unlinked profile within the group, no longer connected to any account.
  • Matches you personally created remain in your group's history (deleting your account does not delete matches or scores), but they are no longer attributed to your account as the creator.

What is retained, and why

Match results, scores, and statistics are shared data belonging to your group, not to any one account — deleting your login does not remove your group's shared match history. Your player profile record is kept (unlinked from any login) specifically so that your former groupmates' rankings, standings, and match history stay accurate and complete.

If you have questions about account deletion, contact couchleagueapp@gmail.com.
