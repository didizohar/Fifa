Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Couch League ("we", "us", "the app") collects, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have. Couch League is a companion app for tracking football video-game matches, rankings, and statistics within private friend groups.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect the email address and password you provide. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and is never visible to or stored by us in plain text.

Profile and player information

For each player profile you create (yourself or a friend within your group), we store a display name, an optional nickname, an optional profile color, and an optional profile picture that you choose to upload. Player profiles also track gameplay statistics (see "Match and session information" below).

Groups and group membership

We store the groups you create or join, your role within each group (owner, admin, or member), and an invite code used to let others join a group.

Match and session information

We store the matches you record: scores, which players and clubs were involved, results, and timestamps. We also store "Winners Stay" session state (who's currently playing, who's waiting) and season/league configuration for your group. Statistics and rankings (such as Elo ratings, league tables, and leaderboards) are calculated from this match data.

User-uploaded images

If you choose to set a profile picture for a player, the image you select is uploaded to our storage provider and a link to it is stored with that player's profile.

Information we do not collect

Couch League does not include any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software, and does not request access to your device's location, contacts, or camera roll beyond the single image you actively choose to select when setting a profile picture.

Why we collect this information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
  • To let you create or join a group and play sessions with your friends.
  • To record match results and calculate the rankings, statistics, and history the app is built around.
  • To let you personalize your player profile.

How your information is used

Your information is used only to provide the app's core functionality described above. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

How your information is stored

Your account, profile, group, and match data is stored in a hosted database and file storage provided by Supabase (see "Service providers" below). Some preferences that are purely local to your device — such as your chosen language and a few display preferences — are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Service providers

We use Supabase as our backend platform, providing authentication, our database, and file storage for uploaded profile pictures. Supabase processes this data on our behalf to operate the app and has its own privacy and security practices as an infrastructure provider. We do not use any other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking service in Couch League.

Data sharing within a group

Match results, rankings, statistics, and player profiles are shared among the members of the group they belong to — that's the purpose of the app. This information is not visible to people outside your group.

Data retention

We retain your account and group data for as long as your account exists, so that your group's match history, rankings, and statistics remain available. If you delete your account, we follow the deletion process described in "Account and data deletion" below.

Security

We restrict access to your data using authentication and database-level access-control policies, so that each user can only read or modify data belonging to a group they are a member of. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You can access and update most of your information directly within the app (your player profile, group memberships, and match records). You can request a copy of your data, or ask us a question about what we hold, by contacting us at the email address below.

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app, without contacting us: Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your login credentials. Because match history, rankings, and statistics are shared with other members of your group, your player profile and the matches you've played are retained but unlinked from your account rather than deleted outright, so your former groupmates' history stays accurate. Full details are on our Account Deletion page.

Children's privacy

Couch League is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at couchleagueapp@gmail.com.
