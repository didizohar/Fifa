Couch League Support

Need help with Couch League? We're here to help.

Account and login issues

Couch League accounts are created and signed in to with an email address and password. If you can't sign in, use "Forgot password?" on the login screen to receive a password reset email. If you never received the confirmation or reset email, check your spam folder first, then contact us below.

Creating or joining a group

Every player belongs to a group. After signing up, you can create a new group (you become its owner) or join an existing one using the invite code shared by a group owner or admin. Both options are available from onboarding and again later from Settings.

Sessions and recording matches

A "Winners Stay" session tracks a full evening of matches for 2, 3, or 4+ players, with automatic rotation between rounds. You can record a match from the full Record Match screen or from the compact "Save Result" card on the Dashboard. Both save to the same match history and update the same session automatically.

Rankings and statistics

The League Table, Leaderboards, and Trends screens are calculated automatically from your group's recorded match history — there's nothing to configure manually. If a number looks wrong, check whether the underlying match result needs to be corrected from the match's detail screen.

Club draws

Club draws pick random clubs for a match by strength (e.g. large/small club pools), or let you reuse or swap the clubs from the previous match. These are available both from the full Record Match screen and from the Dashboard's quick-match card.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your Couch League account at any time from inside the app — no need to contact support. See the Account Deletion page for exactly what happens and what is retained.

Reporting a bug

If something isn't working as expected, please email us with as much detail as you can: what you were doing, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and your app version if you know it. A screenshot helps a lot.
